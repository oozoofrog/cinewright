A rectangular cell holds surface water W, mobile pigment M per channel, and deposited pigment P. Each undirected adjacent pair has closed boundaries: water flux `dt*Dw*(Wa-Wb)` and pigment flux `dt*Dp*(Ma-Mb) + waterFlux*upwind(M/W)`; zero water gives zero concentration. This is a simplified conservative diffusion and advective transport model, not a Navier–Stokes or calibrated paper simulation.
