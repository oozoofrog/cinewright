# C representative image inspection

This records model inspection of generated PNGs, not continuous human viewing, an independent review or aesthetic acceptance.

- Initial station frame 96: threshold, traveler and authored long floor shadow are separated. Surface hatch remains legible against the physical/gobo shadow bands. Initial frame191: train appeared as an undetailed rectangular volume because its windows were on the opposite side. Added cab windows, belt, lamps, bumper and camera-side windows in authored JSON; no renderer demo branch. Final frame191 reads as a stylized arriving train; actor remains visible on the platform.
- Final station frame72 at1080p: the long graphic shadow extends toward camera from the opening while neither actor nor train is visible. Frame104 and191 preserve the same set. Hatching is intentionally conspicuous, including UV scale differences between long wall/floor panels and smaller props; this is a limitation/authoring choice, not physically uniform engraving density.
- Initial laboratory frame96: extreme yaw made the five leaves read as a tight single bulb. Adjusted authored yaw/spread/width; final frame96 shows a fan with separate upper and side leaves, central veins and branch lines. Curved tips, broad light bands and finer attached hatch are visible. Veins are rigid authored lines; the separate narrow shoot alone uses rest deformation.
- Effects frame96: curved sphere, flat box, bending leaf and original animated vessel/seed are independently visible. Sphere shows discrete toon bands and an outer shell; box shows its authored diagonal line. UV frequency depends on each primitive's UV parametrization and is not a global world-distance measure.
- Mixed frame96: Canvas ring is centered at the camera-projected specimen base; gold line/leader contrast is deliberately subtle on the lit table. Existing number channel simultaneously drives light strength and annotation. It is a diagrammatic screen annotation, not a depth-occluded 3D line.

Successful automated comparisons at sampled reverse/random frames establish same-browser pixel consistency. They do not alone prove continuous-motion taste, perceptual flicker acceptability, cross-GPU equality or suitability for an unrelated imported mesh. Remaining limitations and authoring ranges are in the style guide.

- Portrait frame96: plant is deliberately enlarged; right-side foliage and vessel are cropped, while the pot, broad veins and tabletop remain visible. This is an authored close framing, not automatic layout.
